How to Use the Admin Panel

Clinical Rules

Clinical rules tell Claude how to make decisions when analyzing medical records. These are your explicit instructions that override general AI behavior.

Categories:

  • general - Rules that apply to all cases
  • age - Age-specific rules (elderly patients, etc.)
  • treatment_history - Rules based on prior treatments (ESI, RFA history)
  • diagnosis - Diagnosis-specific rules (myelopathy, cauda equina)
  • body_part - Region-specific rules (cervical, lumbar, etc.)

Priority:

Higher numbers = applied first. Use 100-150 for general guidelines, 200-250 for important rules, 300+ for critical overrides.

Example Rules

Rule: "Cervical Herniated Disc - Conservative First"

Category: body_part

Subcategory: cervical

Condition: Patient has cervical herniated disc without prior surgery

Action: Recommend C1 scenario (non-operative management) first. Include imaging surveillance every 2 years and annual spine specialist follow-up. Only recommend surgical consultation if 6+ months of conservative care fails.

Priority: 200

Rule: "ESI Failure - No More Than 3 Series"

Category: treatment_history

Condition: Patient has had 3 or more ESI series without sustained relief

Action: Do not recommend additional ESI. Consider surgical consultation or spinal cord stimulator evaluation instead.

Priority: 250

# Global Principles for Life Care Plan Recommendations

## Principle 1: Structural vs Non-Structural Classification

**Structural injuries require lifelong surveillance:**
- Disc herniations (cervical, thoracic, lumbar)
- Disc bulges with annular fissures
- Fractures (vertebral, extremity)
- Labral tears (shoulder, hip)
- Rotator cuff tears
- Meniscus tears
- Ligament tears (ACL, PCL)
- Spondylolisthesis

**Non-structural injuries resolve and do NOT require long-term care:**
- Sprains
- Strains
- Contusions
- Muscle spasms (without structural cause)

## Principle 2: Treatment Based on Documented Benefit

Only include treatments that:
1. Patient has already tried AND documented benefit, OR
2. Are clinically indicated but not yet tried (with clear justification)

Never include speculative treatments without documented need.

## Principle 3: Conservative Before Invasive

Treatment progression:
1. Medications, PT, home exercises
2. Injections (ESI, joint injections)
3. Minimally invasive procedures
4. Surgery (only if conservative fails)

## Principle 4: Structural Spine Injury Surveillance Bundle

For ANY structural spinal injury (herniation, bulge with fissure, fracture, spondylolisthesis):

| Item | Frequency | Duration |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Spine Specialist Follow-up (99214) | 1x/year | Life expectancy |
| MRI of affected region | Every 2 years | Life expectancy |
| X-ray of affected region | Every 2 years | Life expectancy |
| Physical Therapy (97110, 97140) | 24 visits every 5 years | Life expectancy |

## Principle 5: Frequency Interpretation

| Frequency Text | Annual Calculation |
|----------------|-------------------|
| "Yearly" or "1x/year" | unit cost × 1 |
| "2x/year" or "2 times per year" | unit cost × 2 |
| "Every 2 years" | unit cost ÷ 2 |
| "Every 3 years" | unit cost ÷ 3 |
| "Every 5 years" | unit cost ÷ 5 |
| "24 visits every 5 years" | (unit cost × 24) ÷ 5 |
| "One time" | $0 annual, full cost in one-time column |

## Principle 6: Professional + Facility Fee Rules

| Service Type | Fee Components |
|--------------|----------------|
| Office visits (99xxx) | PFR only |
| Physical therapy (97xxx) | PFR only |
| Diagnostic imaging (MRI, X-ray, CT) | PFR global fee (NO modifier 26) |
| Procedures (injections) | PFR + (APC × geo multiplier) |
| Surgery (inpatient) | PFR + (DRG × geo multiplier) |

## Principle 7: Age Calculations

- **Age Initiated:** Patient's current age at time of report
- **Until Age:** Age Initiated + Life Expectancy (rounded to whole number)
- Exception: Some conditions have shorter durations (e.g., concussion monitoring 5 years)

## Principle 8: Rationale Requirements

Each item rationale MUST:
1. Be patient-specific (reference actual findings)
2. Include dates from medical records
3. Be 1-2 sentences maximum
4. Never be generic boilerplate

**Good example:**
"Annual surveillance of L3-4, L4-5, L5-S1 disc herniations with moderate stenosis per 7/10/25 MRI."

**Bad example:**
"Standard of care for this condition."

## Principle 9: ESI Recommendations

| Scenario | Recommendation |
|----------|----------------|
| Radiculopathy < 1 year, no prior ESI | One-time ESI |
| Radiculopathy, had ESI with benefit | ESI series (3/year, every 2-3 years) |
| No documented radiculopathy | No ESI |

## Principle 10: Concussion/TBI Duration

| Severity | Monitoring Duration |
|----------|---------------------|
| Mild concussion with full recovery | 1-2 years |
| Mild-moderate with persistent symptoms | 3-5 years |
| Moderate-severe TBI | Life expectancy |
